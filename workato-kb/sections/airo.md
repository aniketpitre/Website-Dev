# Section: airo (19 pages)

- `docs/airo.md` — **AIRO**: AIRO is Workato's AI automation engineer that transforms intent into production-ready business solutions.
- `docs/airo/acumen.md` — **Acumen**: Acumen monitors your Workato workspace, raises active incidents, assists with troubleshooting, and forecasts usage.
- `docs/airo/blueprints.md` — **Blueprints**: Blueprints transform business requirements into implementation plans. AIRO recommends the Workato asset that fits each requirement.
- `docs/airo/blueprints/create.md` — **Create your first blueprint**: Create your first blueprint from business requirements, using an employee onboarding example.
- `docs/airo/blueprints/edit.md` — **Edit your blueprint**: Edit a blueprint after it's generated by renaming or restructuring assets, then review, apply, and sync your changes.
- `docs/airo/blueprints/manage.md` — **Manage blueprints**: Rename, delete, sort, and filter blueprints in your workspace.
- `docs/airo/blueprints/requirements-best-practices.md` — **Best practices for writing requirements**: Structure requirements in chat or a PRD or BRD so they map to the right assets.
- `docs/airo/build/custom-connectors.md` — **Build custom connectors with AIRO MCP**: Build, review, save, and release Workato custom connectors from Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or Cursor through the AIRO MCP server.
- `docs/airo/build/recipes.md` — **Build recipes with AIRO**: Describe a use case in plain language and AIRO builds or modifies the recipe in the editor.
- `docs/airo/chat.md` — **Chat with AIRO**: Chat with AIRO from anywhere in Workato. AIRO retains conversation context across pages.
- `docs/airo/chat/chat-history.md` — **Manage AIRO chat history**: Revisit, rename, and delete past conversations with AIRO from the chat panel or the All chats list.
- `docs/airo/homepage.md` — **Homepage**: The AIRO homepage is the default landing page and includes an input bar, active incidents, recent work, and shortcuts for creating assets.
- `docs/airo/knowledge-bases.md` — **What AIRO knows**: AIRO uses your workspace assets and Workato platform knowledge to answer questions.
- `docs/airo/knowledge/manage.md` — **AIRO Playbooks**: Upload, preview, search, and delete playbooks that AIRO references when answering questions.
- `docs/airo/mcp.md` — **AIRO MCP server**: Connect an MCP-compatible AI client to the AIRO MCP server with OAuth 2.0 or an API token to build and manage Workato assets in natural language.
- `docs/airo/usage-inspiration/automate-to-do-list.md` — **Automate your to-do list with AIRO**: Use AIRO to build a solution that turns meeting notes into Trello cards and a Slack summary.
- `docs/airo/usage-inspiration/declutter-inbox.md` — **Declutter your inbox with AIRO**: Use AIRO to build a solution that sorts a Gmail inbox into Slack alerts and labeled folders.
- `docs/airo/usage-inspiration/find-unused-assets.md` — **Find unused assets with AIRO**: Use AIRO to find idle recipes, disconnected connections, and unused genies or knowledge bases in your workspace.
- `docs/airo/usage-inspiration/form-to-slack-alert.md` — **Build a form-to-Slack alert with AIRO**: Use AIRO to build a solution that sends a Slack alert when someone submits a Google Form.
