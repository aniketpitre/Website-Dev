# Workbot for Microsoft Teams

[Video](https://youtube.com/embed/lw2Fgd0FsZY)

[Workbot for Microsoft Teams](https://www.workato.com/platform/workbot?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=workbot) is a Workato chatbot that helps you monitor and interact with your business apps from within Microsoft Teams.

Your team can configure Workbot to post messages to Microsoft Teams users and channels when important events happen. For example, you can configure Workbot to respond when a key customer files a ticket, a lead from a Fortune 500 company fills in a form on your website, or when a DevOps alert is created.

You can also retrieve, create, and update data from your apps from within Microsoft Teams by sending commands to Workbot.
