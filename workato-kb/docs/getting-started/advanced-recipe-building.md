# Advanced recipe building

In this section, learn how to build complex workflows with advanced recipe logic.

## Manage the flow of your recipe with conditional logic

Use [IF conditions](/en/features/if-conditions) to manage how your recipe runs depending on the situation.

## Work efficiently with list

Learn how to manage the [list datatype in your recipe](/en/features/list-management).

## Speed up recipes with batch processing

Increase the throughput of your recipes with [Batch processing](/en/features/batch-processing). Run recipes that handles batches of records at a time. The batch size depends on the specific action/trigger used.
