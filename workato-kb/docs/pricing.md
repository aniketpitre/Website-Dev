# Billing and usage

Workato uses a usage-based billing model. This section covers how billing works, how to measure and estimate usage, and how to manage it across your workspace.

- [Overview](/en/pricing/overview): Learn how Workato's usage-based billing model works, including how costs accumulate across capabilities.
- [Understand usage](/en/pricing/usage-metrics): Learn how Workato measures usage for each product capability and how usage builds up across a job, a use case, and your workspace.
- [Estimate usage](/en/pricing/estimate): Work through example use cases to project usage before you build.
- [Monitor usage](/en/pricing/billing-dashboard): Track usage consumption across your workspace.
- [Self-Service](/en/pricing/self-service/self-service): Sign up for Workato Free or Workato Pro directly online.
- [Billing and Usage FAQs](/en/pricing/pricing-faqs): Find answers to frequently asked questions about Workato pricing.

> The usage and billing structure described in this guide applies only to customers who are part of the usage-based billing model. If you joined Workato before February 2024, you are part of a separate model. Refer to your organization's billing plan and contract, or contact your Customer Success Representative to learn more.
