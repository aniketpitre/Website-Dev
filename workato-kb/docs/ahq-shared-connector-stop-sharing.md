# Stop sharing a connector

To stop sharing a connector, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to your Workato account.
   
2. Go to **Automation HQ**.
   
3. Select the **Shared connectors** tab.
   
4. Find the connector you plan to stop sharing.
   
5. Click the **ellipsis** icon of your connector.
   
6. In the **Stop sharing connector** dialog, click on the title of the modal.
   
   _Stop sharing the connector shared connector_
   
   To cancel this action, click outside the dialog modal.
   
7. The confirmation dialog appears.
   
8. Under the title **Are you sure?**, click **Stop sharing**.
   
   _Stop sharing the connector_
   
   If you don't want to stop sharing the connector, click **Cancel**. This takes you back to the previous prompt in the dialog.
   

Done. The shared connector no longer appears on the **Shared connectors** list.
