> Use Control plane to scale a workspace-level identity across an organization with multiple workspaces.

# Set your preferences

You can customize your setting preferences within Control plane.

## Choose a theme

Complete the following steps to choose a theme:

1. Go to your Control plane account.
   
2. Click your profile icon to view the **Theme** settings.
   
   ![Theme settings](/assets/theme-settings.Syfpr5Il.png)_Theme settings_
   
3. Click the theme setting you plan to use. Options are **Light**, **Dark**, and **System**.
