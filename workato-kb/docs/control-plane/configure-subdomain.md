# Configure subdomain

You must configure a subdomain to provide end users with access to the Control plane.

Complete the following steps to configure your subdomain:

1. Sign in to your Workato account.
   
2. Expand the workspaces menu.
   
3. Click **Control plane**.
   
4. Sign in using your Control plane credentials or SSO.
   
5. Go to **Settings**.
   
6. Click **Subdomain** in the side navigation bar.
   
7. Enter a unique subdomain in the **Subdomain** field.
   
   ![Enter a unique subdomain in the Subdomain field](/assets/configure-subdomain.D5i2qXGx.png)_Enter a unique subdomain in the **Subdomain** field_
   
8. Click **Save changes**.
