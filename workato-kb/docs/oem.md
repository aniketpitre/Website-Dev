# Embedded

Workato's Embedded platform provides a powerful tool for building and managing product integrations directly within your software. Embedded enables you to use Workato's robust architecture to create a seamless end-user experience. This approach offers a significant advantage over developing an in-house integration or automation solution, saving time and reducing complexity.

## Embedded features

Embedded enables you to leverage Workato's comprehensive suite of integration tools to enhance your product's functionality. These integration tools allow you to manage, monitor, and deploy solutions across thousands of customers through a unified administrative experience. This simplifies the support process, enabling field teams to assist users effectively throughout the customer journey.

Key benefits include:

- **Immediate insights**: Gain real-time feedback on customer issues and usage metrics, allowing for prompt and informed decision-making.
- **Enhanced product value**: Integrate enterprise-ready automation features directly into your product, empowering your customers to maximize the utility and value of your solutions.
- **Customized user experiences**: Use Workato's multi-tenancy architecture to create tailored customer journeys. Secure iframes ensure that integrations are not only powerful but also secure.

Embedded enables you to give your customers the tools they need to succeed directly within your product's user interface. This enhances user engagement and enriches the functionality of your offerings.
