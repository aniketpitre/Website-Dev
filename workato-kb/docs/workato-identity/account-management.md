> Set up your Workato ID, sign in with a password or SSO, reset your password, and unlock your account with Workato Identity end user account management.

# End user account management

Workato Identity enables you to manage your Workato ID account. You can set up your Workato ID account, access an invitation to a workspace feature, such as genie, reset your password, and unlock your account.

## Set up your Workato ID

You must manually set up your Workato ID account if the system doesn't recognize your email address.

Complete the following steps to set up your Workato ID:

1. Go to your email and open the **Set up your Workato ID** email.
   
2. Click **Get started**.
   
3. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
   
   ![Set up your Workato ID](/assets/set-up-workato-id.FMXjQY-c.png)_Set up your Workato ID_
   
4. Enter your email address in the **Email address** field.
   
5. Enter the password you plan to use in the **New password** field. Your password must be at least 8 characters in length, contain upper and lower case letters, a number, and include a special character.
   
6. Enter your password again in the **Confirm password** field.
   
7. Click **Continue**.
   
8. Click **Done** when your Workato ID is set up.
   
   ![Click Done](/assets/workato-id-done.CBDNecI0.png)_Click **Done**_
   

## Sign in with your Workato ID

Complete the following steps to sign in with Workato ID to access a feature, such as a genie:

1. Go to your Workato ID login page.
   
2. Select your sign-in method:
   
   **Sign in with a password**
   
   1. Enter your email address in the **Email address** field.
      
   2. Enter your password in the **Password**.
      
   3. Click **Log in**.
      
   
   **Sign in with SSO**
   
   1. Sign in to your organization's SSO login page when prompted.
      
   

## Reset your Workato ID password

You can reset your password from the Workato ID login page.

Complete the following steps to reset your password:

1. Go to your Workato ID login page.
   
2. Click the **Forgot password?** link.
   
3. Enter your email address in the **Email address** field.
   
   ![Reset password request](/assets/reset-password.C5B4sa6Y.png)_Enter your email address_
   
4. Click **Send email**.
   
5. Go to your email and open the message from Workato.
   
6. Click **Reset your password**.
   
   ![Reset password email](/assets/reset-password-email.BPcz1dV2.png)_Click **Reset your password**._
   
7. Follow the prompts to reset your password.
   

## Unlock your Workato ID account

Your Workato ID account is automatically locked after too many unsuccessful login attempts. An email is sent with unlock instructions when you are locked out of your Workato ID account.

Complete the following steps to unlock your account:

1. Go to your email and open the message from Workato.
   
2. Click **Unlock your account**.
   
   ![Unlock your account](/assets/unlock-your-account.C_wOZUru.png)_Click **Unlock your account**._
   
3. Follow the prompts to reset your password.
