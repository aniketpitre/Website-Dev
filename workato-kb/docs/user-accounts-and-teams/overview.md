## Managing Account Privacy

- [Enable two-factor authentication](/en/security/two-factor-authentication) on your account
- Protect your data with [data masking](/en/features/data-masking)
- Control your data flow with [retention policies](/en/security/data-protection/data-retention/)
- Approve Workato on your network with [IP allowlists](/en/security/ip-allowlists)
- Sign in using [Single-sign On](/en/signing-up/sso)
