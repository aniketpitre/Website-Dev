> Learn about Insights dashboards, including the workspace overview and project overview templates that give visibility into your workspace.

# Dashboards

Insights dashboards are designed to provide an overview of your automations' functionality out of the box and include two templates pre-configured with queries to give you insights into your workspace and projects:

- [Workspace overview](#workspace-overview)
- [Project overview](#project-overview)

Alternatively, you can start with a blank template to build your dashboard from scratch.

## Workspace overview

The **Workspace overview** template comes pre-configured with queries that provide a high level overview of your workspace, task usage, and high usage recipes/projects.

**High level numbers**

![Workspace dashboard overview](/assets/workspace-dashboard-1.CYi_Gfo_.png)_High level numbers_

Gain a high level understanding of how your workspace is being used across all environments.

**Task usage over time**

![Workspace dashboard task usage](/assets/workspace-dashboard-2.CSqzeNHf.png)_Task usage over time_

See how consumption evolves over time to make sure there are no outstanding issues and everything is on track. By default, graphs display trends over the past 365 days. No value means that the underlying recipe or project has been deleted.

**High usage recipes/projects**

![Workspace dashboard high usage](/assets/workspace-dashboard-3.XWUziGsz.png)_High usage recipes and projects_

Investigate which recipes and projects consume the most tasks in your workspace during the current calendar year.

## Project overview

The **Project overview** template comes pre-configured with queries that provide a high level overview of a project you select, including task usage, and recipe job runs.

**Project overview**

![Project dashboard overview](/assets/project-dashboard-1.CWBvnSnK.png)_Project overview_

Gain a high-level overview of the project(s) in your workspace, including data recipe, task, and job data.

**7-day overview**

![Project dashboard 7-day overview](/assets/project-dashboard-2.DUo_HeWA.png)_7-day overview_

See a breakdown of the last 7 days of recipes runs in this project, by recipe and result.

**Usage trends**

![Project dashboard usgae trends](/assets/project-dashboard-3.Bq7oOyLP.png)_Usage trends_

Track task consumption trends and highest usage recipes.
