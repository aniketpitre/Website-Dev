> How to configure role-based access control

# AgentX Support - Permissions and roles

You can assign users different roles based on the apps and functions they need to access in AgentX Support.

Each app supports the following roles:

- **Builder**: Full access to the app. Builders can create, edit, and save all elements within the app.
- **Viewer**: Read-only access. Viewers can see all elements in the app but can't make changes.
- **Analytics**: Access to usage data and reports generated by the app.
- **Publisher**: Permission to publish changes to AI agents and flows, including guide flows.

You can also assign the **Admin** role to users to grant additional site-level permissions, including:

- Adding and managing users and their roles
- Connecting third-party integrations
