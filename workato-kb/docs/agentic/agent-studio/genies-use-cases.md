# Agent Studio example use cases

You can use Agent Studio to build customized genies capable of handling your workflows. Genies can be designed to perform the following roles:

### Logistics coordinator genie

You can create a logistics coordinator genie to streamline supply chain operations by tracking shipments, managing inventory updates, and coordinating delivery schedules.

#### Key capabilities

The following features enable logistics coordinator genies to perform their roles effectively:

- **Shipment tracking**: Monitor real-time shipment statuses and notify stakeholders of delays or delivery confirmations.
- **Inventory updates**: Sync with warehouse systems to provide current stock levels and trigger reorders when thresholds are met.
- **Delivery scheduling**: Coordinate delivery windows with carriers and customers based on availability and route optimization.
- **Exception handling**: Detect and flag anomalies like lost items or delays for human review.
- **Carrier performance insights**: Analyze trends to identify high-performing and under-performing logistics partners.

### Data analyst genie

You can create a data analyst genie to help teams explore trends, generate reports, and make informed decisions by automating data collection and interpretation tasks.

#### Key capabilities

The following features enable data analyst genies to perform their roles effectively:

- **Automated data aggregation**: Pull data from multiple sources, such as APIs, spreadsheets, and databases and standardize it for analysis.
- **Trend detection**: Use built-in logic or models to surface anomalies, outliers, or recurring patterns in business metrics.
- **Custom report generation**: Automatically compile insights into presentation-ready dashboards or scheduled reports.
- **Interactive querying**: Respond to natural language questions with contextual insights, charts, or metrics.
- **Goal tracking**: Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and alert stakeholders when values fall outside of expected ranges.

### R&D workflow genie

You can create an R&D workflow genie to support research and development teams by organizing experiments, tracking results, and generating reproducible workflows.

#### Key capabilities

The following features enable R&D workflow genies to perform their roles effectively:

- **Experiment logging**: Record structured inputs, methods, and outcomes for ongoing tests or research.
- **Version control integration**: Track changes to research models, datasets, or documentation.
- **Collaboration coordination**: Assign tasks or reviews to specific researchers and track completion.
- **Insight extraction**: Summarize trends or patterns from past experiments.
- **Report automation**: Generate shareable summaries or results for stakeholders or regulatory bodies.
