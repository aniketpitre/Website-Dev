# AI Marketplace

The [AI Marketplace](https://marketplace.workato.com/en/) is Workato's curated catalog of enterprise AI solutions. Workato Genies, MCP servers, solution accelerators, and partner-built solutions are in one place so you can adopt a solution today instead of building from scratch.

> **WORKATO GENIES AND AGENT STUDIO**
> 
> Workato Genies offer proven skills and patterns for specific business functions, providing a foundation to accelerate genie development. [Agent Studio](https://docs.workato.com/en/agentic/agent-studio) allows you to [create custom genies](https://docs.workato.com/en/agentic/agent-studio/create-a-genie).

## What's in the AI Marketplace

- **[Workato Genies](/en/agentic/workato-genies)**: Pre-built AI agents that autonomously execute end-to-end business processes. For example, a Rep Genie handles call follow-ups and CRM updates so account executives can focus on selling.
- **[MCP servers](/en/mcp/mcp-servers)**: Prebuilt MCP servers that expose enterprise systems and workflows to any MCP-compatible AI agent or assistant.
- **Solution accelerators**: Ready-to-deploy templates that provide solutions for common enterprise AI use cases.
- **Partner-built solutions**: Offerings from Workato's partner ecosystem that extend the catalog across industries and departments.

## Why use the AI Marketplace

The AI Marketplace shortens time to value for organizations. You can discover, evaluate, and scale AI solutions from one hub instead of scoping, building, and testing solutions from the ground up or navigating a fragmented ecosystem of vendors.

Visit the [AI Marketplace](https://marketplace.workato.com/en/) to browse available solutions.
