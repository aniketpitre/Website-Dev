# Workato Genies

Workato Genies are purpose-built AI agents designed to automate specific business functions. Each genie accelerates development with purpose-built skills, knowledge bases, and app integrations designed for common business workflows.

> **WORKATO GENIES AND AGENT STUDIO**
> 
> Workato Genies offer proven skills and patterns for specific business functions, providing a foundation to accelerate genie development. [Agent Studio](/en/agentic/agent-studio) allows you to [create custom genies](/en/agentic/agent-studio/create-a-genie).

You can customize and extend Workato Genies to fit your organization's specific requirements.

Workato Genies are organized by business function.

## HR Genies

The following genies help HR teams and employees handle routine HR requests in chat.

[

### HR Self Service Genie

Gives employees and managers conversational access to Workday for time-off requests and approvals, profile and organization lookups, holiday calendars, HR policy answers, and HR tickets.



](/en/agentic/workato-genies/hr/hr-self-service-genie.html)

## IT Genies

The following genies help IT and operations teams automate support, manage software licenses, and monitor EDI transactions.

[

### EDI Genie

Delivers end-to-end visibility across EDI transactions for teams using Orderful, with natural-language access to transaction data, trading partner health, audit reports, and proactive alerts.



](/en/agentic/workato-genies/it/edi-genie.html)[

### IT Support Genie

Helps employees and IT teams resolve IT issues by automating helpdesk tasks including knowledge base queries, access provisioning, password resets, ticket management, and group management.



](/en/agentic/workato-genies/it/it-support-genie.html)[

### License Genie

Helps IT and finance teams reduce software costs by analyzing usage patterns, identifying underutilized licenses, and coordinating approval workflows for license optimization.



](/en/agentic/workato-genies/it/license-genie.html)

## Sales Genies

The following genies automate sales operations processes, including quote generation and administrative work.

[

### CPQ Genie

Automates quote creation and contract amendments in Salesforce CPQ through guided workflows that enforce business rules and validation.



](/en/agentic/workato-genies/sales/cpq-genie.html)[

### Rep Genie

Automates sales workflows including call follow-up, CRM updates, call preparation, account planning, coaching insights, and daily digests for reps and managers.



](/en/agentic/workato-genies/sales/rep-genie.html)
