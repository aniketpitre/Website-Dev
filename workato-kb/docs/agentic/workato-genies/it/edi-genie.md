# EDI Genie

EDI Genie is a packaged genie for Workato EDI (powered by Orderful) that gives EDI analysts, integration managers, and compliance teams natural-language access to transaction data, trading partner health, and audit reports. It reduces the manual effort behind troubleshooting and compliance reporting.

## What EDI Genie does

EDI Genie supports EDI and operations teams across five use cases:

- **Transaction retrieval and analysis**: Retrieve and filter EDI transactions by date, partner, type, and status using natural language.
- **Trading partner performance and error monitoring**: Aggregate partner metrics, error trends, latency, and SLA risk into conversational insights.
- **Audit, alerts, and operational insights**: Auto-generate audit reports, detect anomalies, and deliver proactive operational alerts.
- **Transaction failure and poller diagnostics**: Surface failing or stuck transactions with root cause data and remediation guidance.
- **Onboarding and setup guidance**: Answer onboarding and setup questions by retrieving relevant guides from a curated knowledge base.

## Connected systems

EDI Genie connects to Orderful through the Workato EDI connector and delivers results through your existing communication tools:

- **EDI platform**: Orderful
- **Chat interface**: Slack (preconfigured). Microsoft Teams and Workato GO are supported with additional setup.
