# Workato GO mobile app

The Workato GO mobile app brings AI-powered automation to your iOS device. This enables you to maintain productivity when away from your desk. The Workato GO mobile app provides essential capabilities for mobile productivity. You can search across your company's connected applications, interact with custom genies, and review recent conversations.

> **MOBILE APP AVAILABILITY**
> 
> The Workato GO mobile app requires iOS 16 or later and a workspace in the US data center.

![Workato GO mobile app home screen](/assets/mobile-app-view.CQsfOtx_.png)_Workato GO mobile app home screen_

## Search

The mobile app search feature scans your company's internal tools and public resources to return relevant results from sources like Google Drive, Slack, Confluence, and Salesforce. Search includes the following abilities:

- **Context-aware routing**: Workato GO determines whether a query is served by a genie, a search result, or both.

Search enables you to find specific information, retrieve documents, surface updates, and initiate tasks. For example:

- `Most recent sales proposal for Acme`
- `Q2 planning`
- `I need to create a Google slide for a new prospect`

## Chat

You can interact with genies and Enterprise data through chats. Workato GO enables you to attach photos from your gallery, capture images with your device camera, or upload files from your file system.

![Workato GO mobile app chat with genie view](/assets/chat-with-genie-mobile-view.D2pLcnEf.png)_Workato GO mobile app chat with genie view_ The chat view displays a conversation with a genie, including attached images and files. ![Chat with a genie in mobile app](/assets/mobile-chat.D_jwLrpB.png)_Chat with a genie in the mobile app_

### Chat history and threads

You can access your chat history under **Recents** and continue threads started on your desktop.

![Access chat history under Recents](/assets/mobile-sidebar.B3g9nqaQ.png)_Chat history_

## Download Workato GO mobile app

Complete the following steps to download the Workato GO mobile app:

1. Go to the App Store on your iOS device.
   
   > **MOBILE APP AVAILABILITY**
   > 
   > The Workato GO mobile app requires iOS 16 or later and a workspace in the US data center.
   
2. Search for Workato GO mobile app and tab **Get**.
   
3. Tap **Open** when the download completes.
   

## Limitations

Workato GO mobile app doesn't support the following desktop features:

- Action Board
- MCP tools within Workato GO
- Find
- Suggested links
- Calendar
- Directory organization charts and people
- User profile
- Genies page
- Personal settings
- Admin
