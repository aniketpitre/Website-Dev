# Set your preferences

You can customize your setting preferences within Workato GO.

## Choose a theme

Complete the following steps to choose a theme:

1. Go to your Workato GO account.
   
2. Click your profile icon in the side navigation bar. The **Preferences** page displays automatically.
   
3. Locate the **Theme** section and select the theme radio button you plan to use. Options are **Light theme**, **Dark theme**, and **Sync with device**.
