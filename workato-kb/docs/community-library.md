# Community library

The community library allows you to find and install platform assets, such as community recipes, community connectors, and [skills](/en/agentic/agent-studio/create-a-genie#create-skills) created by other builders. These pre-built solutions can be customized to fit your needs, helping you quickly integrate and orchestrate automations without starting from scratch.

You must agree to Workato's [community policy](https://www.workato.com/legal/community) to use the community library.

> **FEATURE AVAILABILITY**
> 
> The Community library isn't accessible to workspaces in the China data center.
> 
> Customers using the China data center must build custom solutions or work with the Professional Services team to develop recipe templates, custom connectors, and best practices. [Recipe lifecycle management (RLCM)](/en/recipe-development-lifecycle#recipe-lifecycle-management) features are supported for the China data center. This means that users can deploy assets to higher environments and import or export `.zip` files to their workspaces. This reflects local regulatory requirements and applies to our multi-tenant and [Virtual Private Workato (VPW)](/en/cloud-hosting-editions/hosting-editions#virtual-private-workato) offerings.

## Browse assets

The community library has integrated filters and search to help you find assets that are relevant to your [use case](/en/getting-started/workato-use-cases). Popular use cases include AI/Machine Learning, Finance and Accounting, Operations, and more.

You can filter assets by use case or by the applications they use.

**Filter assets by use case**

![Filter assets by use case](/assets/filter.Bk3RWUZK.gif)_Filter assets by use case_

**Filter assets by application**

![Filter assets by application](/assets/filter-by-app.BJ1tR-9m.gif)_Filter assets by application_

You can toggle filters in the community library's sidebar or enter them in the search bar.

To add filters to the search bar:

- Enter `app:` or `category:` followed by the app or category name.
- Put quotation marks around the app or category name if it contains a space.
- Separate each new filter with a space.

The following example shows how to use filters in the search bar:

```
app:Workday app:"Google drive" category:Utility
```

## Permissions

You must have specific [privileges](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/role-based-access/new-model/privileges-reference) to perform the following actions in the community library:

- Installing connectors
  
- [Full access to the Connector SDK](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/role-based-access/new-model/privileges-reference#connector-sdk) is required to install connectors from the community library.
  
- Cloning recipes
  
- [Recipe creation](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/role-based-access/new-model/privileges-reference#project-assets) permissions are required to make copies of recipes from the community library.
  
- Uploading connectors
  
- [Full access to the Connector SDK](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/role-based-access/new-model/privileges-reference#connector-sdk) is required to upload connectors to the community library.
  
- Uploading recipes
  
- [Recipe editing](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/role-based-access/new-model/privileges-reference#project-assets) permissions are required to upload recipes to the community library.
