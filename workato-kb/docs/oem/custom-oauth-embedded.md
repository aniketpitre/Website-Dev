# Custom OAuth profiles for Embedded partners

Custom OAuth profiles for Embedded partners enable you to create your own custom apps on supported connectors and connect them to Workato. This gives you control over the app's branding, permission scopes, and OAuth profile.

You may plan to create a custom OAuth profile for security reasons in situations where data cannot flow through an app not belonging to you, or if you have permissions to scopes on your own applications that Workato does not support.

As an Embedded partner, you can register your applications so that the OAuth consent form does not appear as 'Workato' requesting permissions. Instead, you can configure the form to match your organization, providing a branded experience for your customers.

Custom OAuth profiles are available for Embedded partners for the following apps:

- Google Drive
- Google Sheets
- [HubSpot](/en/oem/custom-oauth-embedded-create)
- OneDrive
- Outlook
- Microsoft Dynamics
- Microsoft Sharepoint
- Salesforce
- Salesforce (secondary)
- Zoom

In addition, the same OAuth profiles available to direct users are available to Embedded partners. This includes:

- [Slack](/en/custom-oauth-profiles/slack)
- [Workbot for Slack](/en/workbot/workbot-custom-bots)
- [Workbot for Microsoft Teams](/en/workbot-for-teams/guides/creating-enterprise-workbot)
