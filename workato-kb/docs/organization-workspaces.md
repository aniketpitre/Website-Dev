# Organization and workspaces

Workspaces are essential for structuring and managing automation projects within your organization. By providing a well-organized workspace, you set your builders up for success, enabling them to collaborate effectively and build automations tailored to your business needs.

## Workspaces

A workspace for an organization group in Workato allows multiple users to collaborate on project-specific workflows while remaining operationally independent from other workspaces. This setup helps organize recipes, connections, and other assets into meaningful groups for every department, level of business, or organizational unit.

Learn how [workspaces support collaboration between members](/en/workspace).

## Workspace provisioning

When you establish your partnership with Workato, Workato provisions your workspace(s) on the effective start date of your contract. This streamlines user account management, enhances efficiency, reduces errors, and ensures audit compliance.

Learn more about [workspace provisioning](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/workspace-provisioning).

## Control plane

Use Control plane to scale a workspace-level identity across an organization with multiple workspaces. Control plane runs its own SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning independent of any workspace. This allows an enterprise organization to authenticate and auto-provision Control plane users directly against its own IdP. Refer to [Control plane](/en/control-plane) for more information.

## Automation HQ

As Workato scales with your organization, the number of workspaces increases, making organized control essential. Automation HQ offers centralized management and oversight of all automation activities, ensuring consistency and compliance across your entire organization.

Learn how your organization can manage multiple workspaces with [Automation HQ](/en/automation-hq).

## Workspace collaborators

When building integrations for an organization, the scale and complexity of the business processes may result in multiple people working on a set of recipes or even collaborating on a single recipe. With workspace collaborators, multiple users can collaborate while maintaining development and security best practices.

Learn how your organization can collaborate on integrations with [workspace collaborators](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/team-collaboration).
