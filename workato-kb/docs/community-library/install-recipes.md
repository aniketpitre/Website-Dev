# Clone a recipe from the community library

You can clone a recipe from the community library into a project.

> **REQUIRED PERMISSIONS**
> 
> Cloning recipes from the community library requires [recipe creation](/en/user-accounts-and-teams/role-based-access/new-model/privileges-reference#project-assets) permissions. If you don't have the required permissions, contact your workspace admin to help you clone the recipe into your workspace.

Complete the following steps to clone a recipe from the [community library](https://www.workato.com/browse/recipes):

1. Select the community recipe you plan to use.
   
   ![Select a community recipe](/assets/community-recipe.qUee6WCe.png)_Select a community recipe_
   
2. Verify that the recipe fits your use case and assess the quality of the recipe's workflow logic.
   
3. Click **Use this recipe**.
   
   ![Use this recipe](/assets/use-this-recipe.S9hQG99R.png)_Click **Use this recipe**_
   
4. Select the project where you plan to save the recipe, or click **\+ New project** to create a new project.
   
   ![Select where to save the recipe](/assets/where-to-save.DteARbzE.png)_Select where to save the recipe_
   
5. Click **Copy and save** to save a copy of the recipe.
   
   ![Copy and save](/assets/copy-and-save.DJDVxqJk.png)_Click **Copy and save**_
   
6. Click **Customize recipe** to open the recipe editor and begin customizing your recipe.
   
   ![Customize recipe](/assets/customize-recipe.OGGeFWap.png)_Click **Customize recipe**_
