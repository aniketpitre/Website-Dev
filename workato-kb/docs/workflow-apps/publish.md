> Publish a Workflow app to make it available in the Workflow apps portal, and deactivate it to take it offline at any time.

# Publish your app

You must [add users](/en/workflow-apps/app-user-groups#add-users) to the Workflow apps portal and [assign them to your app](/en/workflow-apps/setup-and-access) before you can publish it. Users don't need a Workato account to join the Workflow apps portal.

Click **Go live** to publish your app.

![Publish your app](/assets/app-view-publish.WtoLWt3t.png)_Publish your app_

After you publish your app, click **Go to app** to visit your app in the [Workflow apps portal](/en/workflow-apps/portal-homepage).

## Deactivate an app

You can deactivate an app at any time by clicking **Take offline**. Your app is no longer accessible through the Workflow apps portal or through its URL once it's deactivated.

![App view online](/assets/app-online.DOrKjp7U.png)_Visit your app in the Workflow apps portal_
