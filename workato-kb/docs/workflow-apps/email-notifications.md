> Learn how Workflow apps email notifications alert users about task assignments, completions, expirations, and comments.

# Email notifications

Workato sends app-users email notifications about the following task-related activities:

- When a task is assigned
  
- The user who made the request and the assignees are notified.
  
- When a task is completed (approved/rejected)
  
- The user who made the request and the assignees, except the user who completed the task, receive a notification.
  
- When a task expires
  
- The user who made the request and the task assignees receive a notification.
  
- When a comment is made on a task in the Apps portal
  
- The user who made the request and task assignees receive a notification.
  

* * *

## Disable notifications

Email notifications are enabled by default. App-users can disable notifications in the Workflow apps portal.

Complete the following steps to disable email notifications for Workflow apps:

1. Sign in to the Workflow apps portal.
   
2. Click your user icon and select **Manage your account**. The **Account settings** interface opens.
   
3. Select **Notifications**.
   
4. Use the **Receive email notifications** toggle to disable or enable email notifications.
   
   ![Disable email notifications](/assets/email-notifications-disable.D9IB8zMR.png)_Disable email notifications_
   
5. Click **Save preferences**.
