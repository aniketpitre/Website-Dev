# Workato Academy

[Workato Academy](https://academy.workato.com) offers courses that can teach you how to use Workato effectively, regardless of your technical skill or background. Anyone with a Workato workspace can access these courses and earn certificates upon completion.

## How to access Workato Academy

You can navigate directly to [Workato Academy](https://academy.workato.com) or access it through Workato.

To access Workato Academy through Workato:

1. [Sign in](https://app.workato.com/) to your Workato [workspace](/en/workspace). If you don't have a workspace, you can [create a new workspace](https://app.workato.com/users/sign_up) or [request a free demo workspace.](https://app.workato.com/users/request_demo)
   
2. Click your profile icon and select **Help**.
   
   ![The profile icon menu with Help selected](/assets/help.cXnSzXEA.png)_Open the **Help** panel from the profile icon._
   
3. Click **Workato Academy**.
   
   ![The Help panel with Workato Academy among the options](/assets/help-panel.BFW2a3cR.png)_Click **Workato Academy** in the **Help** panel._
   

## Core training courses

The Workato Foundations series contains Workato Academy's core training courses. The series provides a structured learning pathway with video lessons, study materials, interactive labs, tests, and completion certificates.

The Workato Foundations series consists of the following courses:

- [Workato Foundations 1](https://academy.workato.com/learn/courses/80/workato-foundations-level-1-on-demand): Covers the Workato ONE platform architecture, solution design frameworks, and fundamental recipe design concepts.
- [Workato Foundations 2](https://academy.workato.com/learn/courses/85/workato-foundations-level-2-on-demand): Explores Workato's capabilities across the pillars of our BOAT (Business Orchestration and Automation Technologies) framework, including iPaaS, Data Orchestration, API Management, Business Process Automation (BPA), and AI Agentic workflows.

Workato Academy also offers [specialized courses](https://academy.workato.com/learn/public/catalog/view/20) that focus on specific use cases and industry automation concepts.
