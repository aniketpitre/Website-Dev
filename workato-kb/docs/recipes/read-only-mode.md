# View a recipe

When you open a recipe, you view it without editing, so there's no risk of accidental changes. You can review the recipe's steps, its jobs, and its version history. Click **Edit recipe** to open the recipe in the [recipe editor](/en/recipes/editor) and make changes.

## Recipe

The **Recipe** tab displays the recipe on the canvas. Click any step to view its details. The data tree isn't available while you're viewing the recipe. Click **Edit recipe** to open the recipe editor and make changes.

![A recipe open for viewing](/assets/read-only-mode.D4f4i5KS.png)_A recipe open for viewing_

![View recipe steps](/assets/view-steps.CNhaKdMn.png)_View recipe steps_

Hover over a datapill to see more information about its source.

![Hover to see datapill information](/assets/hover-datapill.DkCggBwt.png)_Hover to see datapill information_

## Jobs

The **Jobs** tab shows your jobs in a customizable format, including the job ID, start date, contact name, and whether the job was a test, production, or repeated job. Click any job to open its job details.

![Job details page](/assets/job-error.YL4QKROt.png)_Job details page_

Refer to [Recipe jobs](/en/recipes/jobs#viewing-job-reports) for more information.

## Versions

Workato saves a version of the recipe each time you save it. You can view and restore previous versions from the **Versions** tab, where each version is denoted by its version number.

![Recipe versions](/assets/recipe-versions.DHU48yW5.png)_Recipe version history page_

Refer to [Recipe version management](/en/recipes/version-management#restoring-versions) for more information.
