# Manage blueprints

Rename, delete, and organize your blueprints in your workspace.

## Rename a blueprint

Complete the following steps to rename a blueprint:

1. Select the three dots next to your blueprint and choose **Rename blueprint**.
   
   ![Rename blueprint](/assets/blueprints-rename.6NR80Oc4.png)_Rename blueprint_
   
2. Enter the new blueprint name and click ✔ to save.
   

## Delete a blueprint

Complete the following steps to delete a blueprint:

1. Select the three dots next to your blueprint and choose **Delete blueprint**.
   
   ![Delete blueprint](/assets/blueprints-delete.CfDkJELW.png)_Delete blueprint_
   
2. Confirm by clicking **Delete blueprint** again.
   

You can recover deleted blueprints from the trash.

> **DELETING BLUEPRINTS**
> 
> Deleting a blueprint does **not** delete the recipes, connections, or other assets it references. These remain available in your workspace.

## Sort and filter blueprints

You can sort blueprints by latest activity or alphabetical order. Use the search bar to filter by name or keyword.
