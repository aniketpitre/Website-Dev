# Declutter your inbox with AIRO

AIRO can sort a cluttered inbox for you, filing newsletters and receipts away from the emails that actually need your attention. Describe the problem in a conversation, and AIRO asks a few clarifying questions before building the solution for you.

Video guide: Build your first project with AIRO

[Video](https://www.youtube.com/embed/WeZw20VMugQ)

> **STEPS MAY VARY**
> 
> AIRO can take a different path to achieve the same goal. The questions it asks and the steps it takes may differ from this example.

## Describe the problem

Start a new conversation with AIRO and describe the problem the way you'd describe it to a coworker:

`My Gmail inbox is out of control. I get so many newsletters, receipts, and notifications every day that I keep missing the emails that actually matter.`

![A chat message describing a cluttered Gmail inbox while AIRO processes the request.](/assets/describe-the-problem.8FRg4c4n.png)_Describe the problem to AIRO_

## Answer AIRO's questions

AIRO asks a few questions to turn your description into a plan:

- **Important emails**: Get notified in Slack, Microsoft Teams, or text. Have emails labeled or starred, or receive a daily digest.
- **Noise**: Auto-archive it, move it into folders, unsubscribe automatically, or leave it alone so only what matters surfaces.
- **What counts as important**: Emails from specific people, emails that need a reply or action, or let AI decide based on content. Combine options. For example, treat emails that need action as important, and let AI decide on everything else.

## Choose a project

AIRO asks where it should build the solution. Create a new project or pick an existing project, then confirm which email platform to use.

## Skip optional assets

AIRO may offer to build additional assets related to your request, such as an asset that lets you ask questions about your inbox from Slack. Skip building the asset if you don't need it yet. You can return to the same conversation later to add it.

## Review the blueprint and connect your apps

AIRO shows you the plan before building anything, also known as a [blueprint](/en/airo/blueprints). The blueprint for this example involves two separate recipes: one recipe alerts you in Slack as soon as an important email arrives, and the other recipe labels and files the rest of the emails so they don't clutter your inbox.

Ask AIRO to walk you through the blueprint, or tell it to change something, before you continue.

AIRO then asks to connect the apps involved, such as Gmail and Slack. Confirm your existing connections or create new connections, then tell AIRO to build.

![The blueprint builder showing two recipes to build, their descriptions, and the connections AIRO will use.](/assets/blueprint.G9Z43kR8.png)_AIRO's blueprint for two recipes_

## Start your recipes

AIRO builds each recipe and adds a note to every step explaining what it does and why. Review and start the recipes to begin sorting your inbox.
