# RicherValues

[RicherValues](https://www.richervalues.com/) is a comprehensive property valuation and inspection platform that provides automated valuation models (AVM), property evaluations, and inspection services for real estate professionals, lenders, and investors. The platform streamlines the property assessment process through digital ordering and reporting systems.

> **API ACCESS REQUIRED**
> 
> You must have an active RicherValues account with API access to connect to RicherValues in Workato. Contact your RicherValues administrator to ensure API access is enabled for your account. Refer to the [RicherValues API documentation](https://doc.richervalues.com/) for detailed information about available endpoints and data structures.

## How to connect to RicherValues on Workato

1. Click **Create > Connection** or press C twice.
   
2. Search for and select **RicherValues** as your connection on the **New Connection** page.
   
3. Provide a unique name for your connection in the **Connection name** field.
   
   ![RicherValues Connection](/assets/richervalues-connection.Dp_xR5hA.png)_RicherValues Connection_
   
4. Select the **Location** of the folder to save your connection.
   
5. Enter the **Username** for your RicherValues account.
   
6. Enter the **Password** for your RicherValues account.
   
7. Click **Connect**.
