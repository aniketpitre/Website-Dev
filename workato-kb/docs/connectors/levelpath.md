# LevelPath

[LevelPath](https://www.levelpath.com/) is a procurement platform designed to streamline supplier onboarding, vendor management, and purchasing operations.

The LevelPath connector lets you integrate LevelPath data into your workflows, enabling real-time supplier events and automated procurement actions.

## How to connect to LevelPath

Workato requires a **Client ID** and **Client secret** from LevelPath to authorize the connection. Complete the following steps to generate credentials and create the connection in Workato:

- [Step 1: Generate API credentials in LevelPath](#generate-credentials)
- [Step 2: Complete the setup in Workato](#complete-setup)

## Generate API credentials in LevelPath

Complete the following steps to retrieve your client credentials:

1. Log in to your LevelPath account as an admin.
   
2. Go to **Company Settings**, then locate your API or integration settings.
   
3. Generate or view your **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
   
4. Copy the credentials and store them securely. You may not be able to view the secret again.
   

## Complete the setup in Workato

1. Click **Create > Connection** or press C twice.
   
2. Search for and select the `LevelPath` connector.
   
3. Enter a name for your connection in the **Connection name** field.
   
   ![Connect to LevelPath](/assets/connect-to-levelpath.CeE407nv.png)_Connect to LevelPath_
   
4. Use the **Location** drop-down to select the project or folder to store this connection.
   
5. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
   
6. Click **Connect**.
