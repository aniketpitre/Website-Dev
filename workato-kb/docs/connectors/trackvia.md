> The TrackVia connector lets you automate data workflows: create, update, and delete records, manage files and users, and trigger on record changes.

# TrackVia

[TrackVia](https://trackvia.com/) is a low-code, cloud-based platform that streamlines workflows and enables companies to manage data, processes, and collaboration in a single environment.

## API version

The TrackVia connector uses the [TrackVia OpenAPI](https://developer.trackvia.com/api-development/swagger).

## How to connect to TrackVia

The TrackVia connector uses OAuth 2.0 authentication to connect to TrackVia.

Complete the following steps to set up your TrackVia connection:

1. Enter a **Connection name** that identifies which TrackVia instance Workato is connected to.
   
2. Enter your **TrackVia subdomain**. Go to **My Account > Advanced Customization > Custom Subdomain** in TrackVia to locate your subdomain. Leave this field blank to use the default subdomain `go.trackvia.com`.
   
3. Enter your TrackVia **Account ID**. Go to **My Account > Account Details > Account Info** in TrackVia to locate your **Account ID**.
   
4. Click **Connect** to open the **TrackVia Public API** authorization dialog. ![TrackVia connection](/assets/trackvia-connect.CDenYWvy.png)_Complete the connection fields and click **Connect**._
   
5. Sign in to your TrackVia account if prompted. If already signed in, proceed to the next step.
   
6. Click **AUTHORIZE** to grant Workato permissions and complete the connection to TrackVia. ![TrackVia authorization](/assets/trackvia-authorize.DbZu8b1N.png)_Click **AUTHORIZE** to complete Workato's connection to TrackVia._
