> The Create customer action in the Jira Service Desk connector lets you create a customer with an email and full name in your service desk instance.

# Jira Service Desk - Create customer action

This action creates a customer in your Jira Service Desk instance.

![Create customer action](/assets/create-customer-action.D0attREU.png)_Create customer action_

## Input

| Input field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Email | Email of the customer |
| Full name | Full name of the customer |

## Output

The output of this action will be a datatree that contains information about the created Jira Service Desk customer.

Find out more about [Jira Service Desk customer object](https://developer.atlassian.com/cloud/jira/service-desk/rest/api-group-customer/#api-rest-servicedeskapi-customer-post).
