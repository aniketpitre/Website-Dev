> The BILL connector lets you automate finance workflows: create, update, and search records, build and send invoices, and trigger on new records.

# BILL

[BILL](https://www.bill.com/) is a cloud-based software platform that automates and simplifies financial processes for small and mid-sized businesses.

## API version

The BILL connector uses the [BILL v2 API](https://developer.bill.com/v2/docs/home).

## How to connect to BILL

The BILL connector uses basic authentication to authenticate with BILL.

Complete the following steps to set up your BILL connection:

1. Enter a **Connection name** that identifies which BILL instance Workato is connected to.
   
2. Enter your BILL account email in the **User name** field.
   
3. Enter your BILL account's **Password**.
   
4. Enter your BILL account's **Organization ID**. To learn how to retrieve your account's **Organization ID**, see BILL's [API sign up process](https://developer.bill.com/v2/docs/sign-up-process#retrieve-organization-id-with-the-api) guide.
   
5. Select whether your BILL account uses the **Production** or the **Sandbox** environment in the **Environment** field.
   
6. Click **Connect**. ![Outlook sign-in dialog](/assets/bill-connect.BXbQJZTC.png)_Completed BILL connection fields and the **Connect** button_
