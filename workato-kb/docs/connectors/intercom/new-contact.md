# Intercom - New contact trigger

The **New contact** trigger activates when a contact is created in Intercom. This trigger initially retrieves contacts created up to one hour before its activation. It then continues to check for new contacts based on the trigger poll interval.

![New contact trigger](/assets/new-contact-intercom.BuGigpNQ.png)_Intercom - New contact trigger_

## Input

- Trigger poll interval
- Determines the frequency of the event check. You can choose a specific interval from the drop-down menu or leave it at the default setting.
